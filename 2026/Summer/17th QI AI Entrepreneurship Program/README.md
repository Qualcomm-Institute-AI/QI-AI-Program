## Overview

The QI AI Entrepreneurship Program is a team-based, hands-on experiential learning program hosted by the Qualcomm Institute (QI) at UC San Diego, introducing participants to the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, software development, and entrepreneurship.

During the Summer 2026 program, seven student teams followed a four-week project cycle from problem discovery through AI model development, software integration, cloud deployment, and final presentation, transforming an initial idea into a functional, cloud-deployed AI service platform. The program concluded with the QI AI Entrepreneurship Hackathon Showcase on July 24, 2026.

## Program Objectives

- Build a foundational understanding of AI and machine learning
- Apply AI and data-driven methods to real-world problems
- Develop AI-powered applications and services
- Integrate AI models into functional, cloud-deployed applications
- Understand the relationship between AI technology and business opportunity
- Collaborate effectively in multidisciplinary teams

## Four-Week Project Cycle

```
Problem Identification & Opportunity Discovery
  → Solution and Product Design
  → AI Model Training & Testing
  → Model Performance Evaluation & Enhancement
  → AI Service Platform Development
  → Model and Application Integration
  → Cloud Deployment
  → Final Presentation and Demonstration
```

## Student Teams

Seven student teams developed AI-powered service platforms addressing different real-world problems and business opportunities.

| Team | AI Service Platform | Project Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Team A | Paragon | Vehicle Drag Coefficient Analysis |
| Team B | VoxShield | Scam Detection & Speaker Analysis |
| Team C | Agrisage | Plant Identification & Health Diagnosis |
| Team D | Dermalyze | Skin Condition & Severity Analysis |
| Team E | Hoteling | Hotel Cancellation Prediction |
| Team F | Solarix | Solar Power Generation Forecasting |
| Team G | D-Avocado | Avocado Ripeness Detection & Prediction |

Final results, awards, and individual team writeups (problem definition, AI/ML approach, technology stack, and repository links) are documented in the [QI AI Entrepreneurship Hackathon – Summer 2026](./QI%20AI%20Entrepreneurship%20Hackathon/README.md) page.
